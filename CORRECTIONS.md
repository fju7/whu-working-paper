# Correction history

## Working Paper v1.0.2 — 2026-09-30

One context-separated Anthropic cross-family AI-agent review and bounded remediation found that the supplied-evidence,
model-acquisition, and no-model synthetic-assurance studies were not commensurate measures of one
capability–assurance effect. The manuscript now presents them as three separate observations that
motivate, but do not answer, a matched capability-versus-assurance research question. It no longer
claims that WHU demonstrated an asymmetry or scaling law.

The revision also adds the frozen synthetic omission prevalences, narrows V3 to bounded descriptive
predictive utility on one deterministic distribution, relabels the economics proposition as an
untested scenario-motivated hypothesis, adds direct references for statistical stopping and
capture–recapture, and distinguishes convergence V2's frozen adequate-share-among-`NOT_ASSURED`
quantity (`512/1,013`) from the V3-aligned false-withholding sensitivity (`512/706`).

No experiment was rerun, no result or prospective disposition was changed, and the unopened
convergence V2 holdout was not accessed. The review and release checks were context-separated
AI-agent reviews, not independent human peer review. Immutable `working-paper-v1.0` and
`working-paper-v1.0.1` remain preserved.
