third-party figures, datasets, or long quotations. The six SVG figures are deterministic WHU-derived
artifacts produced by `tools/build_working_paper_artifacts.py`. Product and model names are used for
identification and attribution, not as a claim of endorsement.

Raw model/provider requests and responses are excluded from the public inventory. That exclusion does
not erase their historical existence; it is recorded in the availability statement and limitations
register.
