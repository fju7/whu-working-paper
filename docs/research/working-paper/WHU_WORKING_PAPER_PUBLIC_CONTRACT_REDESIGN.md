# Working Paper v1.0 public reproduction contract redesign

Date: `2026-09-27`

Disposition: `PUBLIC_REPRODUCTION_CONTRACT_REDESIGNED__RELEASE_GATES_PASS`

The release contract is defined by published science rather than by execution of every internal
repository unit test. The complete prospective classification of the 49-test focused suite is in
`WHU_WORKING_PAPER_PUBLIC_TEST_CLASSIFICATION.md`.

The public package now provides two distinct forms of verification:

1. Result reproduction: V3 threshold selection, logistic fitting, consumed-evaluation metrics, and
   every convergence V2 calibration metric/baseline/ratio/gate recompute from released synthetic
   rows and labels. Figures, the exact-number manifest, and HTML rebuild byte-identically.
2. Provenance/custody integrity: 81 quantitative entries resolve to hash-bound sources; evidence
   statuses are checked; and the V2 holdout commitment, stop disposition, non-result status, and
   exclusion are verified without publishing or deriving the holdout.

The public V2 apparatus is irreversibly calibration-only. The dual-partition builder, original
scorer, full seed/partition recipe, full runtime, partition manifest, and protected holdout labels are
excluded. The strict-tree release verifier rejects unlisted physical files, forbidden V2 paths, and
known V2 holdout-generation signatures.

No manuscript claim, scientific result, evidence label, prior audit, hash history, or custody status
was changed by the redesign.
